The system is a web-based payroll application designed for the Finance Office of
Republic Central Colleges. It automates the computation of employee salaries and
government-mandated deductions — SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR withholding
tax under the TRAIN Law — including the 13th-month pay, and generates digital
payslips and payroll reports that may be downloaded as PDF files.

The development of the system followed the phases of Requirements, Design,
Development, Testing, Deployment, and Review. All computations comply with the
applicable government regulations, namely RA 11199 (SSS), the PhilHealth
contribution schedule, the Pag-IBIG Fund provisions, RA 10963 (TRAIN Law), and
RA 8515 (13th-Month Pay).
